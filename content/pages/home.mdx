## Why learning DeFi alone is hard

You search "what is DeFi" — 40 results. Open YouTube — no idea which channel is credible. Read 3 articles, still don't know where to start.

That's not your fault. **DeFi has no official curriculum.** Every article assumes you already know something else. Nobody tells you the right order.

The result: people jump straight into yield farming before they know how to audit a smart contract. A pool offers 300% APY — they deposit. Three days later the pool is gone.

If you have $5,000–$20,000 sitting in a savings account earning 4–5% APY, DeFi may be worth learning — but only if you know what you're doing.

## FAQ

### Is diverFi free?

All learning content is free. No signup required to read lessons.

### I know nothing about crypto. Can I still learn?

Yes. Track 1 starts from the very basics — what a wallet is, why DeFi is different from a bank. No assumed knowledge.

### Does diverFi tell me what to invest in?

No. diverFi explains how things work and what the risks are. Investment decisions are yours.

### How long does it take to finish?

35 lessons, roughly 15–20 minutes each. At 2 lessons per day, you complete all 4 tracks in about 3 weeks.

### Is DeFi safe?

DeFi has real risks — smart contract bugs, rug pulls, impermanent loss. diverFi doesn't claim DeFi is risk-free. Track 2 (Crypto Security) covers exactly those risks and how to reduce them.
